Primary sources first

Filings, regulators, issuers' own pages and chain reads. Press is labelled and never counted.

Graded and dated

Every figure carries its source, a grade for how firm it is, an as-of date and a definition.

Checked independently

A separate agent that did not make a row checks it against its page before it counts.

Nothing rewritten

History is append-only. Corrections are logged, and gaps are listed rather than filled with guesses.
